# Class 1 Pre-Trip Inspection — Valley Driving School order

> The Class 1 pre-trip is typically completed in **under 45 minutes**.

A faithful transcription of the Valley Driving School *Class 1 Pre-Trip Inspection* sheet,
in its original numbered sequence (steps 1–42). This is the source the in-app
**Pre-Trip Cheatsheet** is built from. Wording and figures are kept as written on the
sheet (e.g. front tread `3 mm`, drive/trailer tread `1.5 mm`, pushrod travel `1/2 – 3/4"`).

> Numbering follows the sheet exactly: steps **7, 8, 9** are the passenger-side repeats of
> **4, 5, 6** (shown as "4 & 7", "5 & 8", "6 & 9"), and the sheet skips index **31**.

---

## Exterior — Start

**Start**

1. **Block wheels** — and confirm emergency equipment: charged fire extinguisher, first aid kit, and safety triangles.
2. **ADIS** — explain ADIS; drain the wet tank if the truck has one.
3. **Hood** — open.

**Under Hood — Driver Side & Passenger Side**

- **4 & 7 · Fluids and levels** — coolant, engine oil, clutch, washer, and steering fluid; adequate level, no leaks.
  - **Engine** — hoses and clamps secure, no cracks and bulges.
  - **Compressor and air lines** — no cracks or leaks.
- **5 & 8 · Belts** — check tension and wear; adequate tension, no cracks.
  - **Steering components / tie rod** — check they are secure with no bends or cracks.
- **6 & 9 · Front brake / wheel axle** — air line, brake chamber, brake pushrod travel (1/2 to 3/4 inch), rim, sidewall, tread (3 mm), inflation, lug nuts, hub oil. If disc brakes, explain there is no pushrod travel.

**Secure**

10. **Hood** — close and secure.
    - **Wet tank** — if no ADIS, close and secure the tank; air lines have no bulges or cracks.

---

## Interior

**In Cab Check**

11. **Key to "on" position** — check warning lights are working and start the engine.
    - **IF NO ADIS** — confirm the one-way check valves are working (air pressure still up).
    - **Start engine** — in neutral, clutch halfway down. Release spring brakes.
    - **Check gauges** — oil pressure, temperature, fuel, and charging system working, and have adequate levels.
12. **Driver / passenger seat and seatbelt** — secure, adjusted, and no frays.
    - **Dash lights and accessories** — turn on lights, hazards, horn, wipers and washer fluid, defroster; confirm working.
    - **Glass** — adjust all mirrors; windows and windshield are clean, no cracks; driver window open and close.

**In Cab Air System & Trailer Breakaway**

13. **Governor unloads compressor** — needles stop climbing, steady pressure, 105–135 psi.
    - **Fan brakes down** — fan down approx. 20 psi, going no lower than 80 psi.
    - **Governor reloads compressor** — air pressure starts climbing.
    - **Low air warning** — fan brakes until warning devices come on, 60 psi or above.
    - **Fan brakes** — until trailer spring brakes dynamite, 45–20 psi.
14. **Shut off engine** — check that spring brakes dynamited on trailer.
    - **Remove glad hands** — no leaks.
    - **Foot brake** — apply 5 sec; tractor protection valve working; listen for leaks.
    - **Reconnect glad hands** — check air lines, electrical cord.
15. **Start engine** — set RPM between 1000–1200.
    - **Time** — building 50 psi to 90 psi, confirm less than 3 minutes.
    - **Release spring brakes** — push tractor and trailer buttons in.
    - **Governor unloads compressor** — build air until governor unloads compressor (105–135 psi).

**Leak Test**

16. **Shut off engine** — ignition to "on" position if electric gauges.
    - **Full brake application** — ensure psi over 90, then apply for 1 minute; start time after gauges settle.
    - **Confirm air loss** — no more than 4 psi within 1 minute.

## Exterior Circle Check

**Lights**

17. **Turn on and check lights** — headlights and left signal.

**Front of Tractor**

18. **Tractor** — bumper, grill, licence plate secure, licence plate valid.
    - **Lenses** — signal, marker, and headlights working.

**Driver Side Tractor**

19. **Mirrors** — secure, checked inside. **Additional lights** — working.
    - **Batteries** — checked weekly for corrosion and leaks. **Drivers door / steps / handrail** — secure, door works.
    - **Fuel and DEF caps** — securely fastened, no leaks. **Exhaust system (pipe and muffler)** — secure, no cracks.

**Driver Side Tractor Axle #2**

20. **Under vehicle** — drive shaft has minimal play; frame not cracked; air bags inflated; differential not leaking; air tanks and air lines secure, no cracks or bulges; brake chambers secure; pushrod travel 1/2 – 3/4".

**Driver Side Tractor Axle #3**

21. **Under vehicle** — short drive shaft has minimal play; air bags inflated; rear differential not leaking; air lines are secure, no cracks or bulges; brake chambers secure; pushrod travel 1/2 – 3/4".

**Rear of Tractor**

22. **Fifth wheel** — jaws are closed. **Licence plate** — secure, valid, light working. **Lights working** — left signal, right park.
    - **Landing gear** — bolt or pin not cracked, raised. **Trailer cross members** — secure, no cracks or bends.
23. **Frame and fifth wheel** — no daylight between plate and bottom of trailer apron.

**Driver Side Trailer**

24. **Tires / wheel / mud flap** — rims not cracked or dented; sidewalls not cracked, cut, or bulged; tire pressure is adequate; lug nut not missing or loose; no rust streaks; tread has even wear, min. 1.5 mm depth; duals clear; mud flap secure and not rubbing or dragging.
25. **Trailer** — no new damage or dents; reflective tape installed.

**Driver Side Trailer Axle #4**

26. **Air brakes** — air tank secure, no leaks; air bags are inflated; air lines have no cracks or bulges; brake chamber secure; slack adjusters pushrod travel 1/2 – 3/4".

**Driver Side Trailer Axle #5**

27. **Air brakes** — air tank secure, no leaks; air bags are inflated; air lines have no cracks or bulges; brake chamber secure; slack adjusters pushrod travel 1/2 – 3/4".

**Driver Side Trailer**

28. **Slider assembly / frame** — no cracks or defects; pins secure and locked.
    - **Tires / wheel / mud flap** — rims not cracked or dented; sidewalls not cracked, cut, or bulged; tire pressure is adequate; lug nuts not missing; no rust streaks; tread has even wear, min. 1.5 mm depth; duals clear; mud flap secure and not rubbing or dragging; hub oil level full (if visible) and no leaks.

**Rear of Trailer**

29. **Reflective tape** — installed. **Load** — open both doors to inspect, close and secure doors.
    - **Lights** — clearance, left signal, tail lights, and licence plate light are working. **Licence plate** — secure, valid sticker.

**Lights**

30. **Brakes** — apply foot brake (ask ICBC Examiner to confirm brake lights are working).
    - **Apply spring brakes** — pull out tractor (yellow) and trailer (red) spring brake buttons.
    - **Turn on and check lights** — right signal and high beams.

**Passenger Side**

32. **Trailer** — no new damage; reflective tape installed.
33. **Slider assembly / frame** — no cracks or defects; pins secure and locked.
    - **Rear trailer tires / wheel / mud flap** — rims not cracked or dented; sidewalls not cracked, cut, or bulged; tire pressure is adequate; lug nuts not missing; no rust streaks; tread has even wear, min. 1.5 mm depth; duals clear; mud flap secure and not rubbing or dragging; hub oil level full (if visible) and no leaks.
34. **Trailer vehicle inspection decal** — secure and valid. **Tractor lights** — right signal working.
    - **Frame and fifth wheel** — no daylight between plate and bottom of trailer apron.
    - **Tractor tires / wheel / mud flap** — rims not cracked or dented; sidewalls not cracked, cut, or bulged; tire pressure is adequate; lug nuts not missing; no rust streaks; tread has even wear, min. 1.5 mm depth; duals clear; mud flap secure and not rubbing or dragging; hub oil level full (if visible) and no leaks.
35. **Exhaust system (pipe and muffler)** — secure and no cracks. **Fuel cap** — securely fastened, no leaks.
    - **Tractor vehicle inspection decal** — secure and valid. **Passenger door / steps / handrail** — secure, door works.
    - **Mirrors** — secure. **Lenses** — signal, marker, headlights.

**Wheel Blocks**

36. **Remove wheel blocks** — confirm spring brakes are applied and remove blocks.

## Brake Response Test

**In Cab**

37. **Start engine** — in neutral, clutch halfway down.
38. **Trailer tug test** — yellow button in, red button out, and truck in first; gently release clutch until resistance is felt.
39. **Tractor tug test** — red button in, yellow button out, and truck in first; gently release clutch until resistance is felt.
40. **Trailer brake** — release both spring brakes, shift to first, gently release clutch, apply trailer hand valve brake.
41. **Tractor brake** — release both spring brakes, shift to first, gently release clutch, apply tractor foot brake.
42. **Trip inspection report** — complete and give to the ICBC Examiner.
