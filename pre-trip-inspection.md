# Class 1 Pre-Trip Inspection

> The Class 1 tractor-trailer pre-trip + air-brake check is typically completed in **under 45 minutes**.

The full walk-around in working order — the source the in-app **Pre-Trip Cheatsheet** is built from. Every line is a single check, numbered **1–92** in sequence so any item can be called out fast (no paired or skipped numbers). Air-brake checks are tagged **(air)**. Figures are kept as written (front tread `3 mm`, drive/trailer tread `1.5 mm`, pushrod travel `1/2 - 3/4"`).

## Air brake numbers

**Test sequence**

| Reading | Trigger | Note |
|---|---|---|
| 105–135 psi | Governor CUT-OUT | compressor stops pumping (724–931 kPa) |
| before 80 psi | Governor CUT-IN | compressor must restart before this (552 kPa) |
| at / above 60 psi | Low-air warning | must activate (414 kPa) |
| 20–45 psi | Trailer air supply valve closes | tractor protection (138–310 kPa) |
| 50 → 90 psi in 3 min | Pressure build-up | at fast idle 1,000–1,200 rpm (345–620 kPa) |
| 90–100 psi | Leak-test application | hold 1 min, engine off (620–690 kPa) |
| ≤ 4 psi / min | Air-loss limit — single trailer | ≤ 6 psi/min with two trailers |
| ≥ 100 psi | Before brake-response tests | reservoir pressure (690 kPa) |

**Limits & thresholds**

| Value | Item | Note |
|---|---|---|
| ~150 psi | Safety (pop-off) valve | vents excess (1,034 kPa) |
| 1¾ in / 45 mm | Applied-stroke limit — manual slack (Type 30) | adjust if exceeded |
| 2 in / 50 mm | Applied-stroke limit — automatic slack (Type 30) | repair if exceeded |
| ½–¾ in / 12–20 mm | Free (pry) stroke — good range | applied good range 1–1½ in / 25–38 mm |
| < ¼ in / 6.4 mm | Drum lining — out of service | air disc pad < ⅛ in / 3.2 mm |
| as low as 20 psi | Tractor protection closes | preserves tractor air (138 kPa) |

## Start

_Set up before anything moves — block the wheels first so the spring brakes can release safely._

1. **Block the wheels** — Chock in front of and behind a tire on the same axle — the vehicle must not move when the spring brakes release.
2. **Emergency equipment** — Charged fire extinguisher, first-aid kit and safety triangles on board.
3. **Air dryer / wet tank** _(air)_ — Explain the automatic drain (ADIS). Drain the wet tank manually if the truck has one.
4. **Open the hood** — Release and raise the hood to begin the engine-compartment checks.

## Exterior — under the hood

_Engine-compartment checks — perform each one on both the driver and passenger sides, then close the hood._

5. **Fluids & levels** — Coolant, engine oil, clutch, washer and power-steering fluid — adequate level, no leaks.
6. **Engine hoses & clamps** — Secure, with no cracks or bulges.
7. **Air compressor & lines** _(air)_ — No cracks or leaks.
8. **Belts** — Adequate tension, no cracks or fraying.
9. **Steering linkage & tie rods** — Secure, with no bends or cracks.
10. **Front wheel & tire** — Rim, sidewall, tread (min 3 mm), inflation, lug nuts and hub oil all good.
11. **Front brakes** _(air)_ — Air line and brake chamber secure; pushrod travel ½–¾ in (12–20 mm). If disc brakes, explain there is no pushrod travel.
12. **Close the hood** — Lower and secure the hood.
13. **Wet tank (if no ADIS)** _(air)_ — Close and secure the tank; air lines have no bulges or cracks.

## Interior — in the cab

_Key on, then start the engine in neutral with the clutch down and work through the cab controls._

14. **Key to ‘on’** — Warning lights illuminate and work, then start the engine.
15. **Check valves (if no ADIS)** _(air)_ — Confirm the one-way check valves hold — air pressure stays up.
16. **Start the engine** — In neutral, clutch halfway down; release the spring brakes.
17. **Gauges** — Oil pressure, temperature, fuel and charging system all working with adequate readings.
18. **Seat & seatbelt** — Driver and passenger — secure, adjusted, no frays.
19. **Lights & accessories** — Headlights, hazards, horn, wipers, washer fluid and defroster all work.
20. **Mirrors** — Adjust all mirrors; secure and clean.
21. **Windows & windshield** — Clean, no cracks; driver window opens and closes.

## Air system & trailer breakaway

_Run the in-cab air-brake sequence — watch the gauge against the air-brake numbers above._

22. **Governor cut-out** _(air)_ — Needles stop climbing; pressure holds at 105–135 psi.
23. **Fan down ~20 psi** _(air)_ — Fan the pressure down about 20 psi, no lower than 80 psi.
24. **Governor cut-in** _(air)_ — Pressure starts climbing again.
25. **Low-air warning** _(air)_ — Keep fanning until the warning device comes on — at or above 60 psi.
26. **Trailer breakaway** _(air)_ — Keep fanning until the trailer spring brakes apply (dynamite) — 45–20 psi.
27. **Shut off the engine** _(air)_ — Confirm the trailer spring brakes applied.
28. **Remove the glad hands** _(air)_ — Disconnect — no leaks.
29. **Foot brake / tractor protection** _(air)_ — Apply ~5 sec; the tractor protection valve works; listen for leaks.
30. **Reconnect the glad hands** _(air)_ — Reconnect and check the air lines and electrical cord.
31. **Restart & set RPM** _(air)_ — Start the engine and set 1,000–1,200 rpm.
32. **Build-up time** _(air)_ — Building 50 → 90 psi must take less than 3 minutes.
33. **Release the spring brakes** _(air)_ — Push the tractor and trailer buttons in.
34. **Build to cut-out** _(air)_ — Build air until the governor cuts out (105–135 psi).

## Applied leakage test

_Engine off, full application, hold for one minute._

35. **Engine off** _(air)_ — Ignition to the ‘on’ position if the gauges are electric.
36. **Full application** _(air)_ — Pressure over 90 psi, then hold the brakes for 1 minute — start timing once the gauges settle.
37. **Confirm air loss** _(air)_ — No more than 4 psi lost in that minute (single trailer).

## Exterior circle check — front

_Start the exterior walk-around at the front, lights on._

38. **Headlights & left signal** — Turn them on, then check them as you walk.
39. **Bumper, grille & plate** — Secure; licence plate present and valid.
40. **Front lenses** — Signal, marker and headlights working, lenses intact.

## Driver side — tractor

_Down the driver side of the tractor, then under the drive axles._

41. **Mirrors** — Secure; checked from inside.
42. **Cab lights & lenses** — Additional lights and lenses working.
43. **Batteries** — Checked weekly for corrosion and leaks.
44. **Door, steps & handrail** — Secure; door works.
45. **Fuel & DEF caps** — Securely fastened, no leaks.
46. **Exhaust system** — Pipe and muffler secure, no cracks.
**Drive axle #2**

47. **Drive shaft & frame** — Drive shaft has minimal play; frame not cracked.
48. **Air bags & differential** — Air bags inflated; differential not leaking.
49. **Air tanks, lines & brakes** _(air)_ — Tanks and lines secure, no cracks or bulges; brake chambers secure; pushrod travel ½–¾ in.
**Drive axle #3**

50. **Drive shaft & frame** — Short drive shaft has minimal play; frame sound.
51. **Air bags & differential** — Air bags inflated; rear differential not leaking.
52. **Air tanks, lines & brakes** _(air)_ — Lines secure, no cracks or bulges; brake chambers secure; pushrod travel ½–¾ in.

## Rear of tractor / fifth wheel

_Confirm the coupling before moving down the trailer._

53. **Fifth wheel coupling** — Locking jaws fully closed and locked around the kingpin.
54. **No daylight at the apron** — No gap between the fifth-wheel plate and the bottom of the trailer apron.
55. **Landing gear** — Fully raised; pin or bolt not cracked.
56. **Cross members** — Trailer cross members secure, no cracks or bends.
57. **Licence plate** — Secure, valid, plate light working.
58. **Rear tractor lights** — Left signal and right park lights working.

## Driver side — trailer

_Down the driver side of the trailer: body, both axles, then the slider._

59. **Trailer body** — No new damage or dents.
60. **Reflective tape** — Conspicuity tape present and intact.
**Trailer axle #4**

61. **Wheels & tires** — Rim, sidewall, pressure, lug nuts (no rust streaks), tread min 1.5 mm, duals clear, mud flap secure.
62. **Air brakes** _(air)_ — Air tank secure, no leaks; air bags inflated; lines sound; brake chamber secure; pushrod travel ½–¾ in.
**Trailer axle #5**

63. **Wheels & tires** — Rim, sidewall, pressure, lug nuts (no rust streaks), tread min 1.5 mm, duals clear, mud flap secure; hub oil full (if visible), no leaks.
64. **Air brakes** _(air)_ — Air tank secure, no leaks; air bags inflated; lines sound; brake chamber secure; pushrod travel ½–¾ in.
65. **Slider assembly & frame** — No cracks or defects; locking pins secure and engaged.

## Rear of trailer

_Inspect the load, then check the brake and signal lights from behind._

66. **Reflective tape** — Conspicuity tape present and intact across the rear.
67. **Cargo doors & load** — Open both doors to inspect the load, then close and secure them.
68. **Rear lights** — Clearance, left signal, tail lights and plate light working.
69. **Licence plate** — Secure, with a valid sticker.
70. **Brake lights** _(air)_ — Apply the foot brake — ask the ICBC examiner to confirm the brake lights work.
71. **Apply spring brakes** _(air)_ — Pull out the tractor (yellow) and trailer (red) spring-brake buttons.
72. **Right signal & high beams** — Turn on and check the right signal and high beams.

## Passenger side

_Back up the passenger side — trailer first, then the tractor — and finish by pulling the blocks._

**Trailer**

73. **Trailer body** — No new damage; reflective tape installed.
74. **Slider assembly & frame** — No cracks or defects; pins secure and locked.
75. **Wheels & tires** — Rim, sidewall, pressure, lug nuts (no rust streaks), tread min 1.5 mm, duals clear, mud flap secure; hub oil full (if visible), no leaks.
76. **Inspection decal** — Trailer vehicle-inspection decal secure and valid.
**Coupling**

77. **No daylight at the apron** — No gap between the fifth-wheel plate and the bottom of the trailer apron.
**Tractor**

78. **Wheels & tires** — Rim, sidewall, pressure, lug nuts (no rust streaks), tread min 1.5 mm, duals clear, mud flap secure; hub oil full (if visible), no leaks.
79. **Exhaust system** — Pipe and muffler secure, no cracks.
80. **Fuel cap** — Securely fastened, no leaks.
81. **Inspection decal** — Tractor vehicle-inspection decal secure and valid.
82. **Door, steps & handrail** — Passenger door, steps and handrail secure; door works.
83. **Mirrors** — Secure.
84. **Lenses** — Signal, marker and headlights.
85. **Right signal** — Tractor right signal working.
**Finish**

86. **Remove wheel blocks** — Confirm the spring brakes are applied, then remove the chocks.

## Brake response test (in cab)

_Final in-cab checks before you hand in the report._

87. **Start the engine** _(air)_ — In neutral, clutch halfway down.
88. **Trailer tug test** _(air)_ — Yellow button in, red button out, truck in first gear; gently release the clutch until resistance is felt — it must not move.
89. **Tractor tug test** _(air)_ — Red button in, yellow button out, truck in first gear; gently release the clutch until resistance is felt — it must not move.
90. **Trailer brake response** _(air)_ — Release both spring brakes, shift to first, gently release the clutch and apply the trailer hand valve.
91. **Tractor brake response** _(air)_ — Release both spring brakes, shift to first, gently release the clutch and apply the tractor foot brake.
92. **Trip inspection report** _(air)_ — Complete it and hand it to the ICBC examiner.
